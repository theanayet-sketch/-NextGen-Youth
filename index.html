About NextGen Youth Society

NextGen Youth Society is a non-profit youth organization dedicated to empowering the next generation of leaders from Bangladesh. Every month we host 4 free professional sessions conducted by top mentors — each session includes a verified certificate.

Our mission is to help Bangladeshi youth through free online mentorship, leadership training, networking opportunities, and career-focused educational sessions — all completely free of cost.

✦4 free professional sessions every month with top mentors
✦Verified certificates issued after each session
✦Leadership, soft skills and career development
